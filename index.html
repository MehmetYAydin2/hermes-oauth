Hermes

A private, single-user personal assistant.

Hermes is a self-hosted personal assistant used by exactly one person — its owner. It connects to that owner's own Google account so it can read and organize email, manage calendar events, and work with files in Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets on the owner's behalf.

What Hermes does with Google access

  • Gmail — reads messages the owner asks about, sends email the owner dictates, and applies labels/read state on request.
  • Google Calendar — lists, creates, and updates events on the owner's calendars.
  • Google Drive, Docs, Sheets — creates and edits files in the owner's own Drive.
  • Contacts (read-only) — looks up addresses when the owner refers to a person by name.

Data handling

All data stays between the owner's Google account and the owner's own server. Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to any third party, and no advertising or analytics services are used. See the privacy policy for details.

Single user

Hermes is not distributed and has no other users. It is used by fewer than 100 people, which Google's OAuth policy treats as personal use.
