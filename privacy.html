Privacy Policy

Hermes — last updated 28 October 2026

Hermes ("the app") is a personal, self-hosted assistant operated by a single individual for their own use. This policy explains what the app accesses and how that information is handled.

Information accessed

When the owner authorizes the app, it receives the scopes needed to:

  • read, send, and label email in the owner's Gmail account;
  • read and modify events in the owner's Google Calendar;
  • create, read, and modify the owner's own files in Google Drive, Google Docs, and Google Sheets;
  • read the owner's contacts, for the sole purpose of resolving names to email addresses.

How information is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner requests in conversation with the assistant — for example, summarizing an email, drafting a reply, listing tomorrow's events, or updating a spreadsheet. The app does not build advertising profiles, does not sell data, and does not use data to train machine-learning models.

Storage and retention

Google OAuth credentials are stored on a server controlled by the owner, with file-level access restrictions. Email and calendar content is processed in memory to fulfill a request and is not persisted except where the owner asks for a summary, note, or file to be kept. The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which no further data is accessed.

Sharing

No data is shared with third parties. The app runs on infrastructure owned and administered by the owner. Requests to Google are made directly from that server over HTTPS.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

The app is not directed to children and has no users other than its owner.

Changes

Any material change to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

Contact: use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
